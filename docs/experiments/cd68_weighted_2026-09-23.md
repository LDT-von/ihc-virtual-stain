# Experiment: CD68-weighted fine-tuning (2026-09-23)

## Hypothesis
CD68 has the largest SSIM gap (+0.120 from target). Per-marker loss weighting
should boost CD68 gradients without changing the architecture.

## Setup
- Init from: `ultimate_w96_expanded/baseline/best.pt` (width=96, 50 epochs, score=75.0876)
- Train on: `roi_split_semifinal_2026_expanded.json` (2100 train, 140 val)
- Clean holdout: ROI019 only (140 patches, never seen during training)
- New loss: per-marker weights applied to SSIM/L1/MSE/multi-scale-L1
- Marker order: (HLA-DR=1.0, CD68=3.0/5.0, CD45RO=1.0, Vimentin=1.0)

## Results on clean ROI019 holdout (TTA=4)

| Variant | CD68 SSIM | Avg SSIM | Avg PSNR | Score | Notes |
|---------|-----------|----------|----------|-------|-------|
| **Baseline w96_expanded** | **0.8156** | 0.8418 | 30.198 | 75.20 | EMA from best.pt |
| weight=3.0, lr=5e-5, epoch=1 (best) | 0.8201 | 0.8413 | 30.260 | 75.20 | +0.0045 CD68 |
| weight=3.0, lr=5e-5, epoch=8 | 0.8151 | 0.8406 | 30.244 | 75.18 | degrades |
| weight=5.0, lr=1e-4, epoch=1 (best) | 0.8230 | 0.8416 | 30.262 | 75.21 | +0.0074 CD68 |
| weight=5.0, lr=1e-4, epoch=4 | 0.8120 | 0.8399 | 30.197 | 75.18 | degrades |

## Key Findings
1. **CD68 SSIM CAN be improved** by per-marker weighting (up to +0.0074)
2. **Other markers degrade slightly** when CD68 weight is high
3. **Peak is at epoch 1**; longer training overshoots and degrades
4. **Net score change ≈ 0** — gain on CD68 is offset by loss on others
5. **Architecture bottleneck**: shared backbone has fixed capacity; CD68 gain
   comes at cost of others

## Insights
- The CD68 gap is NOT due to loss imbalance — it's structural
- CD68 needs either: dedicated head, more capacity, or different supervision
- Per-marker loss weighting gives diminishing returns

## Next Steps (in priority order)
1. **Add CD68-specific auxiliary head** (parallel to 4 main heads, only CD68)
2. **Try w=128** with the SAME training data — more capacity
3. **TTA=8 + multi-model ensemble** of all available checkpoints
4. **Post-processing**: per-marker intensity calibration on holdout
