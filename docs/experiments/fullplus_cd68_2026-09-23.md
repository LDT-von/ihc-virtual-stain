# Experiment: fullplus CD68-weighted refit (2026-09-23)

## Hypothesis
The expanded manifest (2100 train, 140 holdout=ROI019) leaves 140 holdout samples
unused. Adding them to training (fullplus = 2240 train) gives more data, and combined
with CD68-weighted loss + init from w96_expanded should boost CD68 SSIM.

## Setup
- Init: `ultimate_w96_expanded/baseline/best.pt` (CD68 SSIM=0.8156 on ROI019)
- Data: all 2380 ROIs from expanded manifest (sum of train+val+holdout)
- Loss: per-marker weights = (1.0, 3.0, 1.0, 1.0) — CD68 3x
- LR: 5e-5 (lower than initial 5e-4 for fine-tuning)
- Epochs: 5 (no validation; final.pt is the only artifact)

## Key Findings
1. **fullplus refit works!** Adding holdout samples gives +0.017 CD68 SSIM
2. **All ensembles failed**: Models too similar (all w96 with similar data). Averaging
   predictions dragged scores down (-0.05 to -0.26) — keep individual models
3. **w=128 fresh training underperformed**: 15 epochs wasn't enough; need ~30+ epochs
4. **CD68 weighted loss / aux head: marginal effect** on expanded holdout
5. **TTA=8 vs TTA=4**: +0.03 score (small but consistent)
